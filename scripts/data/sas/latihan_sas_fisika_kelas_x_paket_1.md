**Soal 1:**
Two force vectors acting at a single point have magnitudes of $15\text{ N}$ and $7\text{ N}$, respectively. Which of the following values CANNOT be the magnitude of their resultant force?

A. $6\text{ N}$
B. $8\text{ N}$
C. $12\text{ N}$
D. $18\text{ N}$
E. $22\text{ N}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Diketahui:** Besar dua vektor gaya adalah $F_{1} = 15\text{ N}$ dan $F_{2} = 7\text{ N}$.
- **Konsep Fisika:** Besar resultan ($R$) dari dua vektor yang bekerja pada satu titik tangkap bergantung pada sudut apit di antara keduanya. Nilai resultan terkecil (minimum) terjadi ketika kedua vektor berlawanan arah ($\theta = 180^{\circ}$), sedangkan nilai resultan terbesar (maksimum) terjadi ketika kedua vektor searah ($\theta = 0^{\circ}$).
- **Perhitungan Batas Minimum dan Maksimum:**

$$R_{\min} = \vert{}F_{1} - F_{2}\vert{} = \vert{}15\text{ N} - 7\text{ N}\vert{} = 8\text{ N}$$

$$R_{\max} = F_{1} + F_{2} = 15\text{ N} + 7\text{ N} = 22\text{ N}$$

- **Evaluasi Rentang:** Seluruh nilai resultan yang mungkin harus memenuhi pertidaksamaan $8\text{ N} \le R \le 22\text{ N}$. Nilai $8\text{ N}$, $12\text{ N}$, $18\text{ N}$, dan $22\text{ N}$ berada di dalam rentang tersebut, sedangkan $6\text{ N}$ berada di bawah batas minimum ($6\text{ N} < 8\text{ N}$) sehingga mustahil dihasilkan pada sudut berapa pun.

**Jawaban: A**

**Soal 2:**
A hiker walks $60\text{ m}$ due North, then turns West and walks $80\text{ m}$ before stopping. The direction of the hiker's resultant displacement from the starting point is...

A. $30^{\circ}$ North of West
B. $37^{\circ}$ North of West
C. $37^{\circ}$ West of North
D. $53^{\circ}$ North of West
E. $60^{\circ}$ West of North

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Diketahui:** Perpindahan pertama ke Utara $d_{y} = 60\text{ m}$ (sumbu $+y$) dan perpindahan kedua ke Barat $d_{x} = 80\text{ m}$ (sumbu $-x$).
- **Konsep Penamaan Arah Mata Angin (*Bearing*):** Ungkapan "North of West" berarti sudut diukur dari arah Barat (*West*) menuju ke arah Utara (*North*). Sebaliknya, "West of North" berarti sudut diukur dari arah Utara menuju ke Barat.
- **Perhitungan Sudut terhadap Arah Barat ($\theta_{W}$):**
Pada segitiga siku-siku perpindahan, sisi di depan sudut $\theta_{W}$ (terhadap sumbu Barat) adalah komponen Utara ($60\text{ m}$) dan sisi di sampingnya adalah komponen Barat ($80\text{ m}$):

$$\tan \theta_{W} = \frac{d_{\text{Utara}}}{d_{\text{Barat}}} = \frac{60\text{ m}}{80\text{ m}} = \frac{3}{4} = 0,75$$

Berdasarkan tabel trigonometri sudut istimewa, nilai $\tan \theta = \frac{3}{4}$ bersesuaian dengan sudut $\theta_{W} = 37^{\circ}$. Dengan demikian, arah resultannya adalah $37^{\circ}$ ke Utara dari Barat ($37^{\circ}$ North of West). Sebagai catatan, jika diukur dari Utara ke Barat, sudutnya adalah $90^{\circ} - 37^{\circ} = 53^{\circ}$ West of North.

**Jawaban: B**

**Soal 3:**
Two velocity vectors have the exact same magnitude of $v\text{ m/s}$. If the magnitude of their resultant velocity vector is $\sqrt{3}v\text{ m/s}$, what is the angle between the two velocity vectors?

A. $30^{\circ}$
B. $45^{\circ}$
C. $60^{\circ}$
D. $90^{\circ}$
E. $120^{\circ}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Diketahui:** Besar kedua vektor kecepatan sama, yaitu $v_{1} = v$ dan $v_{2} = v$, sedangkan besar kecepatan resultannya adalah $v_{R} = \sqrt{3}v$.
- **Langkah Perhitungan dengan Rumus Kosinus:**

$$v_{R}^{2} = v_{1}^{2} + v_{2}^{2} + 2 \cdot v_{1} \cdot v_{2} \cdot \cos \theta$$

$$(\sqrt{3}v)^{2} = v^{2} + v^{2} + 2 \cdot v \cdot v \cdot \cos \theta$$

$$3v^{2} = 2v^{2} + 2v^{2} \cos \theta$$

Kurangkan kedua ruas dengan $2v^{2}$:

$$v^{2} = 2v^{2} \cos \theta \Rightarrow \cos \theta = \frac{v^{2}}{2v^{2}} = \frac{1}{2}$$

- **Kesimpulan Sudut:** Nilai sudut lancip yang memenuhi $\cos \theta = \frac{1}{2}$ (atau $0,5$) adalah $\theta = 60^{\circ}$.

**Jawaban: C**

**Soal 4:**
Two forces acting on a particle have magnitudes of $15\text{ N}$ and $25\text{ N}$, respectively. If the magnitude of their resultant force is $35\text{ N}$, the angle between the two forces is...

A. $30^{\circ}$
B. $45^{\circ}$
C. $60^{\circ}$
D. $90^{\circ}$
E. $120^{\circ}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Diketahui:** $F_{1} = 15\text{ N}$, $F_{2} = 25\text{ N}$, dan resultan $R = 35\text{ N}$.
- **Langkah Perhitungan dengan Rumus Kosinus:**

$$R^{2} = F_{1}^{2} + F_{2}^{2} + 2 \cdot F_{1} \cdot F_{2} \cdot \cos \theta$$

$$35^{2} = 15^{2} + 25^{2} + 2 \cdot (15) \cdot (25) \cdot \cos \theta$$

$$1225 = 225 + 625 + 750 \cos \theta$$

$$1225 = 850 + 750 \cos \theta$$

$$1225 - 850 = 750 \cos \theta \Rightarrow 375 = 750 \cos \theta$$

$$\cos \theta = \frac{375}{750} = \frac{1}{2} = 0,5$$

- **Kesimpulan Sudut:** Karena $\cos \theta = \frac{1}{2}$, maka sudut apit antara kedua gaya tersebut adalah $\theta = 60^{\circ}$.

**Jawaban: C**

**Soal 5:**
An acceleration vector with a magnitude of $a = 12\text{ m/s}^{2}$ lies in the first quadrant and makes an angle of $60^{\circ}$ with the positive x-axis ($+x$). The components of this acceleration vector along the x- and y-axes are...

A. $a_{x} = 6\text{ m/s}^{2}$ and $a_{y} = 6\text{ m/s}^{2}$
B. $a_{x} = 6\text{ m/s}^{2}$ and $a_{y} = 6\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}^{2}$
C. $a_{x} = 6\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}^{2}$ and $a_{y} = 6\text{ m/s}^{2}$
D. $a_{x} = 6\sqrt{2}\text{ m/s}^{2}$ and $a_{y} = 6\sqrt{2}\text{ m/s}^{2}$
E. $a_{x} = 12\text{ m/s}^{2}$ and $a_{y} = 6\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}^{2}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Diketahui:** Besar percepatan $a = 12\text{ m/s}^{2}$, terletak di Kuadran I (baik komponen $x$ maupun $y$ bernilai positif), dengan sudut $\theta = 60^{\circ}$ terhadap sumbu $+x$.
- **Proyeksi Komponen Sumbu-$x$ (Samping Sudut):**

$$a_{x} = a \cos 60^{\circ} = 12\text{ m/s}^{2} \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = 6\text{ m/s}^{2}$$

- **Proyeksi Komponen Sumbu-$y$ (Depan Sudut):**

$$a_{y} = a \sin 60^{\circ} = 12\text{ m/s}^{2} \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = 6\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}^{2}$$

**Jawaban: B**

**Soal 6:**
A displacement vector $S$ has a magnitude of $25\text{ m}$ and points into the second quadrant (above the negative x-axis), making an angle of $37^{\circ}$ with the negative x-axis. The components of vector $S$ are...

A. $S_{x} = 20\text{ m}$ and $S_{y} = 15\text{ m}$
B. $S_{x} = -15\text{ m}$ and $S_{y} = 20\text{ m}$
C. $S_{x} = -20\text{ m}$ and $S_{y} = 15\text{ m}$
D. $S_{x} = -20\text{ m}$ and $S_{y} = -15\text{ m}$
E. $S_{x} = 15\text{ m}$ and $S_{y} = -20\text{ m}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Diketahui:** Besar perpindahan $S = 25\text{ m}$, berada di Kuadran II dengan sudut lancip $\alpha = 37^{\circ}$ terhadap sumbu $-x$.
- **Analisis Tanda Kuadran II:** Di kuadran kedua (kiri atas bidang Kartesius), proyeksi pada sumbu horizontal mengarah ke sumbu $-x$ sehingga $S_{x}$ bernilai negatif ($-$), sedangkan proyeksi pada sumbu vertikal mengarah ke sumbu $+y$ sehingga $S_{y}$ bernilai positif ($+$).
- **Perhitungan Komponen:**

$$S_{x} = -S \cos 37^{\circ} = -25\text{ m} \times \left(\frac{4}{5}\right) = -20\text{ m}$$

$$S_{y} = +S \sin 37^{\circ} = +25\text{ m} \times \left(\frac{3}{5}\right) = +15\text{ m}$$

**Jawaban: C**

**Soal 7:**
A velocity vector has a magnitude of $16\text{ m/s}$ and is directed at an angle of $-150^{\circ}$ (measured clockwise) relative to the positive x-axis. The horizontal and vertical components of the vector are...

A. $v_{x} = -8\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$, $v_{y} = 8\text{ m/s}$
B. $v_{x} = -8\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$, $v_{y} = -8\text{ m/s}$
C. $v_{x} = -8\text{ m/s}$, $v_{y} = -8\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$
D. $v_{x} = 8\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$, $v_{y} = -8\text{ m/s}$
E. $v_{x} = -8\sqrt{2}\text{ m/s}$, $v_{y} = -8\sqrt{2}\text{ m/s}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Diketahui:** Besar kecepatan $v = 16\text{ m/s}$, sudut arah $\theta = -150^{\circ}$ (diukur searah jarum jam dari sumbu $+x$).
- **Analisis Letak Kuadran dan Sudut Referensi:** Perputaran searah jarum jam sebesar $150^{\circ}$ dari sumbu $+x$ melewati sumbu $-y$ ($-90^{\circ}$) dan menempatkan vektor di Kuadran III (kiri bawah). Sudut lancip (sudut referensi $\alpha$) yang dibentuk vektor terhadap sumbu $-x$ ($-180^{\circ}$) adalah $\alpha = 180^{\circ} - 150^{\circ} = 30^{\circ}$. Di Kuadran III, baik komponen $v_{x}$ maupun $v_{y}$ bernilai negatif ($-$).
- **Perhitungan Komponen Horizontal dan Vertikal:**

$$v_{x} = v \cos(-150^{\circ}) = -v \cos 30^{\circ} = -16\text{ m/s} \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = -8\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$$

$$v_{y} = v \sin(-150^{\circ}) = -v \sin 30^{\circ} = -16\text{ m/s} \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = -8\text{ m/s}$$

**Jawaban: B**

**Soal 8:**
Three coplanar force vectors act simultaneously at the origin $(0, 0)$ of a Cartesian coordinate plane:
- $F_{1} = 110\text{ N}$ directed along the positive x-axis ($+x$),
- $F_{2} = 40\sqrt{3}\text{ N}$ in the second quadrant, making an angle of $30^{\circ}$ above the negative x-axis ($-x$),
- $F_{3} = 40\text{ N}$ in the third quadrant, making an angle of $60^{\circ}$ below the negative x-axis ($-x$).

The resultant of these three force vectors is...

A. $10\text{ N}$ along the negative x-axis ($-x$)
B. $20\text{ N}$ along the negative x-axis ($-x$)
C. $20\text{ N}$ along the positive x-axis ($+x$)
D. $30\text{ N}$ along the negative x-axis ($-x$)
E. $30\text{ N}$ along the positive x-axis ($+x$)

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Catatan Verifikasi Data Soal:** Pada naskah soal asli tertulis $F_{3} = 40\text{ 5}$, yang merupakan salah ketik penulisan satuan untuk $F_{3} = 40\text{ N}$. Hal ini terbukti karena seluruh pilihan jawaban (A sampai E) menyatakan resultan berada murni sepanjang sumbu-$x$, yang mensyaratkan jumlah komponen vertikal sama dengan nol ($\Sigma F_{y} = 0$).
- **Penguraian Komponen Masing-Masing Gaya:**
  - Gaya 1 ($F_{1} = 110\text{ N}$ pada sumbu $+x$):

$$F_{1x} = +110\text{ N} \quad \text{dan} \quad F_{1y} = 0\text{ N}$$

  - Gaya 2 ($F_{2} = 40\sqrt{3}\text{ N}$ di Kuadran II, sudut $30^{\circ}$ di atas sumbu $-x$):

$$F_{2x} = -F_{2} \cos 30^{\circ} = -40\sqrt{3} \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = -20 \times 3 = -60\text{ N}$$

$$F_{2y} = +F_{2} \sin 30^{\circ} = +40\sqrt{3} \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = +20\sqrt{3}\text{ N}$$

  - Gaya 3 ($F_{3} = 40\text{ N}$ di Kuadran III, sudut $60^{\circ}$ di bawah sumbu $-x$):

$$F_{3x} = -F_{3} \cos 60^{\circ} = -40 \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = -20\text{ N}$$

$$F_{3y} = -F_{3} \sin 60^{\circ} = -40 \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = -20\sqrt{3}\text{ N}$$

- **Penjumlahan Komponen pada Sumbu-$x$ dan Sumbu-$y$:**

$$\Sigma F_{x} = F_{1x} + F_{2x} + F_{3x} = 110\text{ N} + (-60\text{ N}) + (-20\text{ N}) = +30\text{ N}$$

$$\Sigma F_{y} = F_{1y} + F_{2y} + F_{3y} = 0\text{ N} + 20\sqrt{3}\text{ N} + (-20\sqrt{3}\text{ N}) = 0\text{ N}$$

- **Kesimpulan Resultan:** Karena $\Sigma F_{x} = +30\text{ N}$ dan $\Sigma F_{y} = 0\text{ N}$, maka resultan ketiga gaya tersebut memiliki besar $30\text{ N}$ dan berarah sepanjang sumbu-$x$ positif (*along the positive x-axis*, $+x$).

**Jawaban: E**

**Soal 9:**
Nadia walks $21\text{ m}$ due East, then turns counter-clockwise by an angle of $127^{\circ}$ (so she heads in the direction $53^{\circ}$ North of West, where $\sin 53^{\circ} = 0.8$ and $\cos 53^{\circ} = 0.6$) and walks $15\text{ m}$ before stopping.
Based on this data, consider the following five statements:
1. The northward component of Nadia's resultant displacement is $12\text{ m}$.
2. The eastward component of Nadia's resultant displacement is $12\text{ m}$.
3. Nadia's resultant displacement magnitude from the starting point is $12\sqrt{2}\text{ m}$.
4. Nadia's resultant displacement magnitude from the starting point is $15\text{ m}$.
5. The direction of Nadia's resultant displacement is $45^{\circ}$ North of East.

The correct statements are...

A. 1 and 2 only
B. 2 and 4 only
C. 1, 2, and 4
D. 1, 3, and 5
E. 1, 2, 3, and 5

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Tetapkan Sistem Koordinat:** Arah Timur (*East*) sebagai sumbu $+x$ dan arah Utara (*North*) sebagai sumbu $+y$.
- **Perpindahan Pertama ($d_{1} = 21\text{ m}$ ke Timur):**

$$d_{1x} = +21\text{ m} \quad \text{dan} \quad d_{1y} = 0\text{ m}$$

- **Perpindahan Kedua ($d_{2} = 15\text{ m}$ dengan arah $53^{\circ}$ Utara dari Barat / Kuadran II):**

$$d_{2x} = -d_{2} \cos 53^{\circ} = -15\text{ m} \times 0,6 = -9\text{ m} \text{ (ke Barat)}$$

$$d_{2y} = +d_{2} \sin 53^{\circ} = +15\text{ m} \times 0,8 = +12\text{ m} \text{ (ke Utara)}$$

- **Evaluasi Kelima Pernyataan:**
  1. Komponen arah Utara (*Northward component*): $R_{y} = d_{1y} + d_{2y} = 0 + 12\text{ m} = 12\text{ m}$ (Pernyataan 1 BENAR).
  2. Komponen arah Timur (*Eastward component*): $R_{x} = d_{1x} + d_{2x} = 21\text{ m} - 9\text{ m} = 12\text{ m}$ (Pernyataan 2 BENAR).
  3. Besar perpindahan resultan: $R = \sqrt{R_{x}^{2} + R_{y}^{2}} = \sqrt{12^{2} + 12^{2}} = \sqrt{144 \times 2} = 12\sqrt{2}\text{ m}$ (Pernyataan 3 BENAR).
  4. Besar perpindahan resultan adalah $15\text{ m}$: Bertentangan dengan hasil perhitungan $12\sqrt{2}\text{ m} \approx 16,97\text{ m}$ (Pernyataan 4 SALAH).
  5. Arah perpindahan resultan: Karena $R_{x} = +12\text{ m}$ (Timur) dan $R_{y} = +12\text{ m}$ (Utara), maka $\tan \theta = \frac{R_{y}}{R_{x}} = \frac{12}{12} = 1$ sehingga $\theta = 45^{\circ}$ ke Utara dari Timur ($45^{\circ}$ North of East) (Pernyataan 5 BENAR).

**Jawaban: E**

**Soal 10:**
An automated warehouse robot starts from its charging station and moves $8\text{ m}$ due North, then steers $8\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$ due East, and finally turns and moves $16\text{ m}$ due South before stopping. What is the magnitude of the robot's resultant displacement from the charging station?

A. $8\text{ m}$
B. $8\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$
C. $12\text{ m}$
D. $16\text{ m}$
E. $24\text{ m}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Diketahui:** Tiga tahapan gerak berurutan: (1) $8\text{ m}$ ke Utara ($+y$), (2) $8\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$ ke Timur ($+x$), dan (3) $16\text{ m}$ ke Selatan ($-y$).
- **Perhitungan Komponen Total Horizontal dan Vertikal:**
  - Komponen Timur-Barat ($x$): $R_{x} = +8\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$
  - Komponen Utara-Selatan ($y$): $R_{y} = +8\text{ m} - 16\text{ m} = -8\text{ m}$ ($8\text{ m}$ ke arah Selatan)
- **Perhitungan Besar Resultan dengan Teorema Pythagoras:**

$$R = \sqrt{R_{x}^{2} + R_{y}^{2}} = \sqrt{(8\sqrt{3})^{2} + (-8)^{2}} = \sqrt{64 \times 3 + 64} = \sqrt{192 + 64} = \sqrt{256} = 16\text{ m}$$

**Jawaban: D**

**Soal 11:**
Two force vectors of equal magnitude, each measuring $15\text{ N}$, act at a common point. If the angle between the two vectors is $90^{\circ}$, determine the magnitude of the resultant vector.

A. $15\text{ N}$
B. $15\sqrt{2}\text{ N}$
C. $15\sqrt{3}\text{ N}$
D. $30\text{ N}$
E. $30\sqrt{2}\text{ N}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Diketahui:** $F_{1} = 15\text{ N}$, $F_{2} = 15\text{ N}$, dan sudut apit $\theta = 90^{\circ}$ ($\cos 90^{\circ} = 0$).
- **Perhitungan Resultan:**

$$R = \sqrt{F_{1}^{2} + F_{2}^{2} + 2 F_{1} F_{2} \cos 90^{\circ}} = \sqrt{15^{2} + 15^{2} + 0} = \sqrt{225 \times 2} = 15\sqrt{2}\text{ N}$$

**Jawaban: B**

**Soal 12:**
A motorboat crosses a $120\text{ m}$ wide river by moving at a constant speed of $8\text{ m/s}$ relative to the water, keeping its bow pointed directly perpendicular to the riverbank (due North). Due to the river current flowing due East, the boat reaches the opposite bank drifted $90\text{ m}$ downstream (to the East) from the point directly opposite its starting position. The speed of the river current and the boat's resultant speed relative to the ground are, respectively...

A. $4\text{ m/s}$ and $8\sqrt{2}\text{ m/s}$
B. $5\text{ m/s}$ and $9\text{ m/s}$
C. $6\text{ m/s}$ and $10\text{ m/s}$
D. $6\text{ m/s}$ and $14\text{ m/s}$
E. $8\text{ m/s}$ and $10\text{ m/s}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Diketahui:** Lebar sungai (arah Utara, sumbu $y$) $\Delta y = 120\text{ m}$, laju perahu terhadap air (arah Utara) $v_{y} = 8\text{ m/s}$, dan jarak pergeseran ke hilir (arah Timur, sumbu $x$) $\Delta x = 90\text{ m}$.
- **Langkah 1 - Hitung Waktu Penyeberangan ($t$):**
Gerak ke arah Utara dan gerak terseret arus ke arah Timur berlangsung dalam selang waktu $t$ yang sama:

$$t = \frac{\Delta y}{v_{y}} = \frac{120\text{ m}}{8\text{ m/s}} = 15\text{ s}$$

- **Langkah 2 - Hitung Laju Arus Sungai ($v_{x}$):**
Selama $t = 15\text{ s}$, arus sungai menyeret perahu sejauh $\Delta x = 90\text{ m}$ ke Timur:

$$v_{x} = \frac{\Delta x}{t} = \frac{90\text{ m}}{15\text{ s}} = 6\text{ m/s}$$

- **Langkah 3 - Hitung Laju Resultan Perahu terhadap Tanah ($v_{R}$):**
Karena kecepatan perahu ($v_{y} = 8\text{ m/s}$ ke Utara) dan kecepatan arus ($v_{x} = 6\text{ m/s}$ ke Timur) saling tegak lurus:

$$v_{R} = \sqrt{v_{x}^{2} + v_{y}^{2}} = \sqrt{(6\text{ m/s})^{2} + (8\text{ m/s})^{2}} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = \sqrt{100} = 10\text{ m/s}$$

**Jawaban: C**

**Soal 13:**
Given two vectors in unit vector notation: $A = 4\hat{i} - 5\hat{j}$ and $B = -2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j}$. The dot product (scalar product) $A \cdot B$ is...

A. $-23$
B. $-7$
C. $7$
D. $17$
E. $23$

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Diketahui:** Komponen vektor $A$ adalah $A_{x} = 4$, $A_{y} = -5$ dan komponen vektor $B$ adalah $B_{x} = -2$, $B_{y} = 3$.
- **Konsep Dot Product Vektor Satuan:** Karena $\hat{i} \cdot \hat{i} = 1$, $\hat{j} \cdot \hat{j} = 1$, dan $\hat{i} \cdot \hat{j} = 0$, maka perkalian titik dua vektor cukup dilakukan dengan mengalikan komponen-komponen yang sejenis lalu menjumlahkannya:

$$A \cdot B = (A_{x} \cdot B_{x}) + (A_{y} \cdot B_{y}) = (4)(-2) + (-5)(3) = -8 + (-15) = -23$$

**Jawaban: A**

**Soal 14:**
A projectile is launched with an initial velocity of $v_{0} = 50\text{ m/s}$ at an elevation angle of $37^{\circ}$ above the horizontal (positive x-axis). The magnitudes of the horizontal component ($v_{0x}$) and the vertical component ($v_{0y}$) of the initial velocity are, respectively...

A. $v_{0x} = 30\text{ m/s}$ and $v_{0y} = 40\text{ m/s}$
B. $v_{0x} = 40\text{ m/s}$ and $v_{0y} = 30\text{ m/s}$
C. $v_{0x} = 25\text{ m/s}$ and $v_{0y} = 25\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$
D. $v_{0x} = 25\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$ and $v_{0y} = 25\text{ m/s}$
E. $v_{0x} = 35\text{ m/s}$ and $v_{0y} = 35\text{ m/s}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Diketahui:** Laju awal $v_{0} = 50\text{ m/s}$ dan sudut elevasi $\theta = 37^{\circ}$ terhadap sumbu horizontal ($+x$), di mana $\cos 37^{\circ} = \frac{4}{5} = 0,8$ dan $\sin 37^{\circ} = \frac{3}{5} = 0,6$.
- **Perhitungan Komponen Horizontal ($v_{0x}$) dan Vertikal ($v_{0y}$):**

$$v_{0x} = v_{0} \cos 37^{\circ} = 50\text{ m/s} \times \left(\frac{4}{5}\right) = 40\text{ m/s}$$

$$v_{0y} = v_{0} \sin 37^{\circ} = 50\text{ m/s} \times \left(\frac{3}{5}\right) = 30\text{ m/s}$$

**Jawaban: B**

**Soal 15:**
Which of the following physical quantities is a vector quantity?

A. Electric current
B. Kinetic energy
C. Momentum
D. Volume
E. Work

---
**Pembahasan:**
- **Konsep Dasar:** Besaran vektor adalah besaran fisika yang memiliki nilai (besar) sekaligus arah serta mengikuti aturan aljabar penjumlahan vektor. Besaran skalar hanya memiliki nilai tanpa arah.

B. Equal to half the product of their magnitudes
C. Zero
D. Negative
E. Undefined

---
**Pembahasan:**
Besar perkalian silang (*cross product*) antara dua vektor $A$ dan $B$ dirumuskan sebagai:
