**Soal 1:**
If a bar magnet is broken or divided into two equal pieces, what happens?

A. the north and south poles are permanently separated.
B. two completely new magnets are formed.
C. the magnetic properties are entirely destroyed.
D. a strong electric field is immediately created.
E. it loses its magnetic field and becomes just a regular piece of metal.

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Konsep Fisika:** Kutub magnet selalu hadir berpasangan sebagai dipol (kutub Utara dan kutub Selatan). Tidak ada kutub magnet tunggal (monopol magnetik) yang dapat dipisahkan secara terisolasi.
* **Proses Fisis:** Ketika sebatang magnet dipatahkan atau dibagi menjadi dua bagian yang sama, susunan magnet elementer (domain magnetik) di dalam kedua potongan tetap sejajar. Pada ujung patahan yang baru akan langsung terbentuk kutub-kutub baru yang berlawanan, sehingga menghasilkan dua buah magnet baru yang utuh (masing-masing memiliki kutub Utara dan Selatan sendiri).

**Soal 5:**
A vertical wire carries an electric current pointing straight down to the ground. To the west of this wire, which way does the magnetic field point?

A. North
B. East
C. South
D. Down
E. Up

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** Arus listrik $I$ mengalir vertikal lurus ke bawah (menuju tanah). Titik pengamatan berada tepat di sebelah Barat (*West*) dari kawat.
* **Penerapan Kaidah Tangan Kanan:** Arahkan ibu jari tangan kanan lurus ke bawah. Apabila dilihat dari atas, lengkungan keempat jari tangan kanan akan berputar searah jarum jam (*clockwise*), yaitu berputar dari $\text{Utara} \rightarrow \text{Timur} \rightarrow \text{Selatan} \rightarrow \text{Barat} \rightarrow \text{kembali ke Utara}$.
* **Arah Garis Singgung di Titik Barat:** Titik di sebelah Barat kawat berada pada posisi jam 9 (dengan Utara di jam 12). Garis singgung lingkaran yang berputar searah jarum jam pada posisi jam 9 mengarah lurus ke atas (menuju jam 12), yaitu ke arah Utara (*North*).

**Soal 11:**
The magnetic force pushing on a current-carrying wire in a uniform magnetic field reaches exactly half (50%) of its maximum possible strength when...

A. the current is parallel to the field lines.
B. the current is at a $30^{\circ}$ angle with respect to the field lines.
C. the current is at a $60^{\circ}$ angle with respect to the field lines.
D. the current is perpendicular (at a $90^{\circ}$ angle) to the field lines.
E. there is no magnetic field present.

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Rumus Dasar:** Besar gaya magnetik adalah $F = B I L \sin\theta$, sedangkan nilai maksimumnya adalah $F_{\text{maks}} = B I L$ (saat $\theta = 90^{\circ}$).
* **Perhitungan Sudut:** Agar besar gaya mencapai tepat setengah ($50\%$ atau $0,50$) dari nilai maksimumnya:
  $$F = 0,50 F_{\text{maks}} \implies B I L \sin\theta = 0,50 (B I L)$$
  $$\sin\theta = 0,50 \implies \theta = 30^{\circ}$$

**Soal 15:**
Two long, parallel wires lie next to each other on a flat table and carry the exact same amount of current, but in opposite directions. The wire on your right has current flowing toward you, and the wire on your left has current flowing away from you. At the exact center point between these two wires, which way does the magnetic field point?

A. points up.
B. points down.
C. points toward you.
D. is zero.
E. points away from you.

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Kawat di Sebelah Kiri (arus menjauhi pengamat / masuk bidang, $\otimes$):** Arahkan ibu jari tangan kanan menjauhi Anda. Keempat jari melingkar searah jarum jam (*clockwise*). Titik tengah berada di sebelah kanan dari kawat kiri (posisi jam 3), sehingga medan magnet $B_{\text{kiri}}$ mengarah ke bawah (*points down*).

Keempat jari melingkar berlawanan arah jarum jam (*counterclockwise*). Titik tengah berada di sebelah kiri dari kawat kanan (posisi jam 9), sehingga medan magnet $B_{\text{kanan}}$ juga mengarah ke bawah (*points down*).
* **Resultan di Titik Tengah:** Karena $B_{\text{kiri}}$ dan $B_{\text{kanan}}$ sama-sama mengarah ke bawah, maka keduanya saling menguatkan dan medan magnet total menunjuk ke bawah (*points down*).

**Soal 17:**
Imagine two circular loops of wire. Both carry the exact same amount of current, but the smaller loop has a radius that is half as big as the larger loop. Compared to the magnetic field at the center of the larger loop, the magnetic field at the center of the smaller loop is...

A. $8$ times stronger.
B. $4$ times stronger.
C. $2$ times stronger.
D. half as strong.
E. exactly the same.

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** $I_{\text{kecil}} = I_{\text{besar}} = I$ dan $R_{\text{kecil}} = \frac{1}{2} R_{\text{besar}}$.
* **Rumus Dasar:** Kuat medan magnet di pusat loop melingkar adalah $B = \frac{\mu_{0} I}{2R}$ sehingga $B$ berbanding terbalik dengan jari-jari $R$ ($B \propto \frac{1}{R}$).
* **Perbandingan:**
  $$\frac{B_{\text{kecil}}}{B_{\text{besar}}} = \frac{R_{\text{besar}}}{R_{\text{kecil}}} = \frac{R_{\text{besar}}}{\frac{1}{2} R_{\text{besar}}} = 2$$
* Jadi, medan magnet di pusat loop yang lebih kecil adalah $2$ kali lebih kuat dibandingkan di pusat loop yang lebih besar.

**Soal 18:**
Think about two circular loops of wire carrying the same amount of current. The larger loop has a radius that is exactly 4 times as big as the smaller loop. Compared to the magnetic moment of the smaller loop, the magnetic moment of the larger loop is...

A. $16$ times stronger.
B. $8$ times stronger.
C. $4$ times stronger.
D. $2$ times stronger.
E. exactly the same.

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** $I_{\text{besar}} = I_{\text{kecil}} = I$ dan $R_{\text{besar}} = 4 R_{\text{kecil}}$.
* **Rumus Dasar:** Momen dipol magnetik loop melingkar adalah $\mu = I A = I (\pi R^{2})$, sehingga $\mu$ berbanding lurus dengan kuadrat jari-jari ($\mu \propto R^{2}$).
* **Perbandingan:**
  $$\frac{\mu_{\text{besar}}}{\mu_{\text{kecil}}} = \left(\frac{R_{\text{besar}}}{R_{\text{kecil}}}\right)^{2} = (4)^{2} = 16$$
* Dengan demikian, momen magnetik loop yang lebih besar adalah $16$ kali lebih kuat.

A proton flies at a speed of $4.0 \times 10^{7}\text{ m/s}$ through a $0.50\text{ T}$ magnetic field. If the angle between the proton's velocity path and the magnetic field lines is $30^{\circ}$, what is the magnitude of the magnetic force acting on it?

A. $8.0 \times 10^{-13}\text{ N}$
B. $1.6 \times 10^{-12}\text{ N}$
C. $3.2 \times 10^{-12}\text{ N}$
D. $6.4 \times 10^{-12}\text{ N}$
E. $1.6 \times 10^{-14}\text{ N}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** $q = 1,60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$; $v = 4,0 \times 10^{7}\text{ m/s}$; $B = 0,50\text{ T}$; $\theta = 30^{\circ}$ ($\sin 30^{\circ} = 0,50$).
* **Perhitungan:**
  $$F = q v B \sin 30^{\circ}$$
  $$F = (1,60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C})(4,0 \times 10^{7}\text{ m/s})(0,50\text{ T})(0,50)$$
  $$F = (6,4 \times 10^{-12})(0,25) = 1,6 \times 10^{-12}\text{ N}$$

**Jawaban: B**

**Soal 27:**
An electron traveling straight north at a speed of $3.0 \times 10^{5}\text{ m/s}$ enters an area where the Earth's magnetic field is $4.0 \times 10^{-5}\text{ T}$. This field points downward at a $45^{\circ}$ angle below the horizontal plane. How much force acts on the electron? (Use $\sin 45^{\circ} \approx 0.707$)

A. $1.4 \times 10^{-18}\text{ N}$
B. $1.9 \times 10^{-18}\text{ N}$
C. $1.4 \times 10^{-19}\text{ N}$
D. $1.9 \times 10^{-19}\text{ N}$
E. $2.7 \times 10^{-18}\text{ N}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** $|q| = 1,60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$; $v = 3,0 \times 10^{5}\text{ m/s}$; $B = 4,0 \times 10^{-5}\text{ T}$; $\theta = 45^{\circ}$ ($\sin 45^{\circ} \approx 0,707$).
* **Perhitungan:**
  $$F = |q| v B \sin 45^{\circ}$$
  $$F = (1,60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C})(3,0 \times 10^{5}\text{ m/s})(4,0 \times 10^{-5}\text{ T})(0,707)$$
  $$F = (4,8 \times 10^{-14})(4,0 \times 10^{-5})(0,707)$$
  $$F = (1,92 \times 10^{-18})(0,707) = 1,35744 \times 10^{-18}\text{ N} \approx 1,4 \times 10^{-18}\text{ N}$$

**Jawaban: A**

**Soal 28:**
An electron shoots forward along the positive x-axis at a speed of $6.0 \times 10^{6}\text{ m/s}$. It enters a $2.0\text{ T}$ magnetic field that points at a $60^{\circ}$ angle to the positive x-axis (lying flat on the xy-plane). Calculate the magnetic force on the electron. (Use $\sin 60^{\circ} \approx 0.866$)

A. $1.7 \times 10^{-10}\text{ N}$
B. $1.9 \times 10^{-10}\text{ N}$
C. $1.7 \times 10^{-12}\text{ N}$
D. $1.9 \times 10^{-12}\text{ N}$
E. $9.6 \times 10^{-13}\text{ N}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** $|q| = 1,60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$; $v = 6,0 \times 10^{6}\text{ m/s}$; $B = 2,0\text{ T}$; $\theta = 60^{\circ}$ ($\sin 60^{\circ} \approx 0,866$).
* **Perhitungan:**
  $$F = |q| v B \sin 60^{\circ}$$
  $$F = (1,60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C})(6,0 \times 10^{6}\text{ m/s})(2,0\text{ T})(0,866)$$
  $$F = (9,6 \times 10^{-13})(2,0)(0,866)$$
  $$F = (1,92 \times 10^{-12})(0,866) = 1,66272 \times 10^{-12}\text{ N} \approx 1,7 \times 10^{-12}\text{ N}$$

**Soal 29:**
Using the exact same situation as the previous question (an electron moving at $6.0 \times 10^{6}\text{ m/s}$ into a $2.0\text{ T}$ magnetic field at a $60^{\circ}$ angle, with $m_{e} = 9.11 \times 10^{-31}\text{ kg}$), calculate the acceleration of the electron.

A. $1.8 \times 10^{18}\text{ m/s}^{2}$
B. $2.1 \times 10^{18}\text{ m/s}^{2}$
C. $1.8 \times 10^{-18}\text{ m/s}^{2}$
D. $2.1 \times 10^{-18}\text{ m/s}^{2}$
E. $3.6 \times 10^{18}\text{ m/s}^{2}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** Dari hasil perhitungan Soal 28, besar gaya magnetik yang bekerja pada elektron adalah $F = 1,6627 \times 10^{-12}\text{ N}$ (atau $1,7 \times 10^{-12}\text{ N}$), dan massa elektron adalah $m_{e} = 9,11 \times 10^{-31}\text{ kg}$.

**Soal 30:**
A proton ($m_{p} = 1.67 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}$, $q = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$) moves at a speed of $2.4 \times 10^{6}\text{ m/s}$ completely perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field. This magnetic force forces the proton to travel in a circle with a radius of $0.10$ meters. What is the magnitude (strength) of the magnetic field?

A. $0.12\text{ T}$
B. $0.25\text{ T}$
C. $0.35\text{ T}$
D. $0.50\text{ T}$
E. $0.75\text{ T}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** Massa proton $m_{p} = 1,67 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}$; muatan proton $q = 1,60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$; laju $v = 2,4 \times 10^{6}\text{ m/s}$; sudut $\theta = 90^{\circ}$; jari-jari lintasan melingkar $r = 0,10\text{ m}$.
* **Hubungan Gaya Magnetik dan Gaya Sentripetal:** Ketika partikel bermuatan bergerak tegak lurus medan magnet seragam, gaya magnetik berperan penuh sebagai gaya sentripetal yang membelokkan lintasan partikel menjadi lingkaran:
  $$F_{B} = F_{\text{sentripetal}} \implies q v B = \frac{m_{p} v^{2}}{r}$$
  Sederhanakan $v$ pada kedua ruas dan susun ulang untuk mencari besar medan magnet $B$:
  $$B = \frac{m_{p} v}{q r}$$
* **Perhitungan Numerik:**
  * Pembilang: $m_{p} v = (1,67 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg})(2,4 \times 10^{6}\text{ m/s}) = 4,008 \times 10^{-21}\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$
  * Penyebut: $q r = (1,60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C})(0,10\text{ m}) = 1,60 \times 10^{-20}\text{ C}\cdot\text{m}$
  * Nilai $B$:
    $$B = \frac{4,008 \times 10^{-21}}{1,60 \times 10^{-20}} = 2,505 \times 10^{-1}\text{ T} \approx 0,25\text{ T}$$

**Jawaban: B**
