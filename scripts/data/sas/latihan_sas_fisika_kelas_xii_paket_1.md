**Soal 1:**
A long cylindrical bar magnet has a north pole at its left end and a south pole at its right end. If a technician cuts this magnet transversely into three equal pieces, what happens to the magnetic poles of the pieces?

A. Only the two outer pieces remain magnetic, while the middle piece becomes neutral iron.
B. The left piece becomes an isolated north pole and the right piece becomes an isolated south pole.
C. Three completely new magnets are formed, each possessing both a north pole and a south pole.
D. All three pieces permanently lose their magnetic properties due to mechanical cutting.
E. An electrostatic charge immediately replaces the magnetic field at the cut surfaces.

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Konsep Fisika:** Di alam tidak pernah ditemukan kutub magnet tunggal (*magnetic monopole*). Sifat kemagnetan suatu bahan feromagnetik berasal dari penyearahan domain-domain magnetik mikroskopis (magnet elementer) di dalam bahan.
* **Proses Fisis:** Ketika sebuah magnet batang dengan kutub Utara ($\text{N}$) di ujung kiri dan kutub Selatan ($\text{S}$) di ujung kanan dipotong melintang (*transversely*) menjadi tiga bagian sama panjang, setiap potongan tetap memiliki susunan domain magnetik yang terarah dari kanan ke kiri. Akibatnya, pada setiap bidang potong akan muncul kutub-kutub baru yang saling berlawanan: potongan kiri menjadi ($\text{N-S}$), potongan tengah menjadi ($\text{N-S}$), dan potongan kanan menjadi ($\text{N-S}$).
* **Evaluasi Opsi:** Opsi A dan D salah karena sifat magnet tidak hilang hanya karena dipotong. Opsi B salah karena mengasumsikan adanya monopol magnetik terisolasi. Opsi E salah karena pemotongan magnet tidak menggantikan medan magnet dengan muatan elektrostatik.

**Jawaban: C**

**Soal 7:**
A circular wire loop carrying a steady current lies flat on a horizontal desk. When viewed from above, the current flows in a clockwise direction. In the plane of the desk, what is the direction of the magnetic field in the region immediately outside the loop?

**Soal 9:**
The standard SI unit of magnetic field strength is the tesla (T). Which combination of SI units is dimensionally equivalent to 1 tesla?

A. $1\text{ N}\cdot\text{A/m}$
B. $1\text{ N}/(\text{A}\cdot\text{m})$
C. $1\text{ N}\cdot\text{m/A}$
D. $1\text{ C}/(\text{s}\cdot\text{m})$
E. $1\text{ J}\cdot\text{A/m}^{2}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Rumus Dasar:** Gaya magnetik pada kawat lurus berarus dirumuskan sebagai $F = B I L \sin\theta$.
* **Penurunan Satuan:** Susun ulang persamaan untuk mencari medan magnet $B$:
  $$B = \frac{F}{I \cdot L \cdot \sin\theta}$$
  Substitusikan satuan SI masing-masing besaran ($F$ dalam newton [$\text{N}$], $I$ dalam ampere [$\text{A}$], $L$ dalam meter [$\text{m}$], dan $\sin\theta$ tanpa dimensi):
  $$1\text{ T} = 1\text{ N}/(\text{A}\cdot\text{m})$$

**Jawaban: B**

**Soal 10:**
A straight conductor carrying a current $I$ is placed inside an external magnetic field $B$ and experiences a non-zero magnetic force $F$. How is the direction of the magnetic force vector $F$ oriented?

A. It is parallel to the current $I$ but perpendicular to the magnetic field $B$.
B. It is parallel to the magnetic field $B$ but perpendicular to the current $I$.
C. It is perpendicular to both the direction of the current $I$ and the direction of the magnetic field $B$.
D. It points at a $45^{\circ}$ angle halfway between the current $I$ and the magnetic field $B$.
E. It always points opposite to the conventional current $I$.

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Konsep Fisika:** Secara vektor, gaya magnetik pada penghantar berarus dinyatakan oleh perkalian silang (*cross product*): $\vec{F} = I \vec{L} \times \vec{B}$.
* **Sifat Perkalian Silang Vektor:** Hasil kali silang dari dua buah vektor selalu menghasilkan vektor ketiga yang tegak lurus (*perpendicular*) terhadap kedua vektor pembentuknya, yaitu tegak lurus terhadap arah arus listrik $I$ sekaligus tegak lurus terhadap arah medan magnet $B$.

**Jawaban: C**

**Soal 13:**
A positively charged ion travels at a constant high speed through a vacuum chamber and experiences zero magnetic force. Based on this observation alone, we can correctly conclude that...

A. the vacuum chamber definitely contains no magnetic field.
B. the ion must be moving perpendicular to the magnetic field lines.
C. a magnetic field is present and the ion moves at a $45^{\circ}$ angle to the field.
D. either no magnetic field is present in the chamber, or the ion is moving parallel (or antiparallel) to the magnetic field lines.
E. the ion lost its mass upon entering the vacuum chamber.

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Rumus Dasar:** Gaya Lorentz pada partikel bermuatan adalah $F = \vert{}q\vert{} v B \sin\theta$.
* **Analisis Kemungkinan Fisis:** Ion bermuatan positif memiliki $\vert{}q\vert{} > 0$ dan bergerak dengan kelajuan tinggi ($v > 0$). Jika teramati $F = 0$, maka terdapat dua kemungkinan logis yang menyebabkan perkalian tersebut bernilai nol:
  1. Tidak ada medan magnet di dalam ruang vakum tersebut ($B = 0$), atau
  2. Ada medan magnet ($B \neq 0$) tetapi ion bergerak sejajar ($\theta = 0^{\circ}$) atau antiparalel ($\theta = 180^{\circ}$) terhadap garis-garis medan magnet sehingga $\sin\theta = 0$.
* **Mengapa Opsi A Kurang Tepat:** Kita tidak bisa memastikan $100\%$ bahwa $B = 0$ hanya dari satu arah lintasan ion tanpa menguji lintasan tegak lurusnya. Oleh karena itu, simpulan ilmiah yang paling akurat adalah opsi D.

**Jawaban: D**

**Soal 15:**
Two long, parallel wires lie side-by-side on a horizontal table and carry equal currents in opposite directions. The left wire carries current pointing straight toward you, and the right wire carries current pointing straight away from you. At the exact midpoint between the two wires, in which direction does the net magnetic field point?

A. It points straight up.
B. It points straight down.
C. It points straight toward you.
D. It is zero.
E. It points straight away from you.

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Kawat Kiri (arus menuju pengamat / keluar bidang, $\odot$):** Gunakan kaidah tangan kanan dengan ibu jari menunjuk ke arah Anda. Keempat jari melingkar berlawanan arah jarum jam (*counterclockwise*). Titik tengah berada di sebelah kanan kawat kiri (posisi jam 3), sehingga vektor medan magnet dari kawat kiri ($B_{\text{kiri}}$) mengarah tegak lurus ke atas (*straight up*).
* **Kawat Kanan (arus menjauhi pengamat / masuk bidang, $\otimes$):** Gunakan kaidah tangan kanan dengan ibu jari menunjuk menjauhi Anda. Keempat jari melingkar searah jarum jam (*clockwise*). Titik tengah berada di sebelah kiri kawat kanan (posisi jam 9), sehingga vektor medan magnet dari kawat kanan ($B_{\text{kanan}}$) juga mengarah tegak lurus ke atas (*straight up*).
* **Superposisi Vektor:** Karena kedua medan magnet di titik tengah sama-sama mengarah ke atas, maka resultan medan magnet ($B_{\text{total}} = B_{\text{kiri}} + B_{\text{kanan}}$) saling menguatkan dan menunjuk tegak lurus ke atas (*straight up*).

**Jawaban: A**

**Soal 16:**
Two long, parallel wires lie side-by-side on a horizontal table and both carry the exact same current pointing straight away from you. At the exact halfway point between these two wires, what is the net magnetic field?

A. It points straight up.
B. It points straight down.
C. It points straight toward you.

Akibatnya, kedua vektor saling meniadakan dan resultan medan magnet bernilai nol (*zero*).

**Soal 17:**
Consider two flat circular loops of wire, Loop X and Loop Y, each carrying the exact same electric current $I$. The radius of Loop Y is three times as large as the radius of Loop X ($R_{Y} = 3R_{X}$). Compared to the magnetic field magnitude at the center of Loop X, the magnetic field at the center of Loop Y is...

A. $9$ times stronger.
B. $3$ times stronger.
C. $1/3$ as strong.
D. $1/9$ as strong.
E. exactly the same.

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** $I_{X} = I_{Y} = I$ dan $R_{Y} = 3R_{X}$.
* **Rumus Dasar:** Besar induksi magnetik di pusat kawat melingkar berjari-jari $R$ adalah:
  $$B = \frac{\mu_{0} I}{2R}$$
* **Perbandingan Kuantitatif:** Karena $\mu_{0}$ dan $I$ bernilai sama, maka $B$ berbanding terbalik dengan jari-jari $R$ ($B \propto \frac{1}{R}$):
  $$\frac{B_{Y}}{B_{X}} = \frac{R_{X}}{R_{Y}} = \frac{R_{X}}{3R_{X}} = \frac{1}{3}$$
* Dengan demikian, medan magnet di pusat Loop Y adalah $1/3$ kali kekuatan medan magnet di pusat Loop X.

**Jawaban: C**

**Soal 18:**
Consider two flat circular loops of wire carrying the exact same electric current $I$. The larger loop has a radius that is three times as big as the smaller loop ($R_{\text{large}} = 3R_{\text{small}}$). Compared to the magnetic dipole moment of the smaller loop, the magnetic dipole moment of the larger loop is...

A. $27$ times stronger.
B. $9$ times stronger.
C. $6$ times stronger.
D. $3$ times stronger.
E. exactly the same.

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** $I_{\text{besar}} = I_{\text{kecil}} = I$ dan $R_{\text{besar}} = 3R_{\text{kecil}}$.
* **Rumus Dasar:** Momen dipol magnetik ($\mu$) dari sebuah loop berarus $I$ dengan luas penampang lingkaran $A = \pi R^{2}$ adalah:
  $$\mu = I \cdot A = I \cdot \pi R^{2}$$
* **Perbandingan Kuantitatif:** Karena arus $I$ sama, maka momen dipol magnetik berbanding lurus dengan kuadrat jari-jari loop ($\mu \propto R^{2}$):
  $$\frac{\mu_{\text{besar}}}{\mu_{\text{kecil}}} = \left(\frac{R_{\text{besar}}}{R_{\text{kecil}}}\right)^{2} = (3)^{2} = 9$$
* Jadi, momen dipol magnetik loop yang lebih besar adalah $9$ kali lebih kuat.

**Jawaban: B**

**Soal 22:**
A thin horizontal copper rod is $0.80$ meters long, has a mass of $0.040\text{ kg}$, and sits perpendicular to a horizontal $0.20\text{ T}$ magnetic field. What minimum electric current is required in the rod so that the upward magnetic force exactly balances the rod's weight? (Use $g = 9.8\text{ m/s}^{2}$)

A. $1.23\text{ A}$
B. $1.96\text{ A}$
C. $2.45\text{ A}$
D. $3.92\text{ A}$
E. $4.90\text{ A}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** $L = 0,80\text{ m}$; $m = 0,040\text{ kg}$; $B = 0,20\text{ T}$; $\theta = 90^{\circ}$ ($\sin 90^{\circ} = 1$); $g = 9,8\text{ m/s}^{2}$.
* **Syarat Kesetimbangan Mekanik:** Agar gaya magnetik ke atas tepat menyeimbangkan berat batang ke bawah ($\Sigma F_{y} = 0$), maka:
  $$F_{B} = W \implies B I L \sin 90^{\circ} = m g$$
* **Perhitungan Arus Listrik ($I$):**
  $$I = \frac{m g}{B L}$$
  $$I = \frac{0,040\text{ kg} \times 9,8\text{ m/s}^{2}}{0,20\text{ T} \times 0,80\text{ m}}$$
  $$I = \frac{0,392\text{ N}}{0,16\text{ T}\cdot\text{m}} = 2,45\text{ A}$$

**Jawaban: C**

A stationary electron sits completely at rest inside a uniform magnetic field of $0.85\text{ T}$. What is the magnitude of the magnetic force acting on this electron?

A. zero
B. $1.36 \times 10^{-19}\text{ N}$
C. $1.60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ N}$
D. $8.50 \times 10^{-20}\text{ N}$
E. $2.72 \times 10^{-19}\text{ N}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** Elektron berada dalam keadaan diam sempurna (*stationary / at rest*), sehingga kelajuannya $v = 0\text{ m/s}$; medan magnet $B = 0,85\text{ T}$.

A proton moves horizontally at a speed of $6.0 \times 10^{4}\text{ m/s}$ into a region where a uniform $0.25\text{ T}$ magnetic field points straight up. What is the magnitude of the magnetic force acting on the proton?

A. zero
B. $1.2 \times 10^{-15}\text{ N}$
C. $2.4 \times 10^{-15}\text{ N}$
D. $4.8 \times 10^{-15}\text{ N}$
E. $9.6 \times 10^{-15}\text{ N}$

  $$F = (1,60 \times 10^{-19})(1,5 \times 10^{4}) = 2,4 \times 10^{-15}\text{ N}$$

**Soal 30:**
A proton ($m_{p} = 1.67 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}$, $q = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$) moves at a speed of $4.8 \times 10^{6}\text{ m/s}$ completely perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field, causing the proton to travel in a circular path of radius $0.25$ meters. What is the magnitude of the magnetic field?

A. $0.10\text{ T}$
B. $0.20\text{ T}$
C. $0.25\text{ T}$
D. $0.40\text{ T}$
E. $0.50\text{ T}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
* **Diketahui:** Massa proton $m_{p} = 1,67 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}$; muatan proton $q = 1,60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$; laju $v = 4,8 \times 10^{6}\text{ m/s}$; sudut $\theta = 90^{\circ}$; jari-jari lintasan melingkar $r = 0,25\text{ m}$.
* **Hubungan Gaya Lorentz dan Gaya Sentripetal:** Ketika partikel bermuatan bergerak tegak lurus medan magnet seragam, gaya magnetik berperan penuh sebagai gaya sentripetal yang membelokkan lintasan partikel menjadi lingkaran:
  $$F_{B} = F_{\text{sentripetal}} \implies q v B = \frac{m_{p} v^{2}}{r}$$
  Sederhanakan $v$ pada kedua ruas dan susun ulang untuk mencari besar medan magnet $B$:
  $$B = \frac{m_{p} v}{q r}$$
* **Perhitungan Numerik:**
  * Pembilang: $m_{p} v = (1,67 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg})(4,8 \times 10^{6}\text{ m/s}) = 8,016 \times 10^{-21}\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$
  * Penyebut: $q r = (1,60 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C})(0,25\text{ m}) = 0,40 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}\cdot\text{m} = 4,0 \times 10^{-20}\text{ C}\cdot\text{m}$
  * Nilai $B$:
    $$B = \frac{8,016 \times 10^{-21}}{4,0 \times 10^{-20}} = 2,004 \times 10^{-1}\text{ T} \approx 0,20\text{ T}$$

**Jawaban: B**
