**Soal 1:**
Two force vectors have magnitudes of $10\text{ N}$ and $4\text{ N}$, respectively. Which of the following CANNOT be the magnitude of their resultant force?

A. $5\text{ N}$
B. $6\text{ N}$
C. $10\text{ N}$
D. $12\text{ N}$
E. $14\text{ N}$

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** Besar kedua vektor gaya adalah $F_{1} = 10\text{ N}$ dan $F_{2} = 4\text{ N}$.
* **Perhitungan Nilai Minimum dan Maksimum Resultan:**

$$
R_{\min} = \vert{}F_{1} - F_{2}\vert{} = \vert{}10\text{ N} - 4\text{ N}\vert{} = 6\text{ N} \text{ (saat } \theta = 180^{\circ}\text{)}
$$

$$
R_{\max} = F_{1} + F_{2} = 10\text{ N} + 4\text{ N} = 14\text{ N} \text{ (saat } \theta = 0^{\circ}\text{)}
$$

* **Evaluasi Rentang:** Besar resultan $R$ harus memenuhi $6\text{ N} \le R \le 14\text{ N}$. Oleh karena itu, nilai $5\text{ N}$ tidak mungkin dihasilkan karena lebih kecil daripada batas minimum $6\text{ N}$.

**Jawaban: A**

**Soal 2:**
A person walks $80\text{ m}$ to the east, then turns south and walks $60\text{ m}$. The direction of the person's displacement is...

A. $60^{\circ}$ south of east
B. $60^{\circ}$ east of south
C. $53^{\circ}$ south of east
D. $37^{\circ}$ south of east
E. $37^{\circ}$ east of south

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** Komponen perpindahan ke Timur $d_{\text{Timur}} = 80\text{ m}$ (sumbu $+x$) dan komponen perpindahan ke Selatan $d_{\text{Selatan}} = 60\text{ m}$ (sumbu $-y$). Vektor perpindahan resultan berada di Kuadran IV (Tenggara).
* **Perhitungan Sudut terhadap Arah Timur (*South of East*):**
  Jika sudut diukur dari poros Timur menuju ke arah Selatan (*south of east*), maka sisi depan sudut adalah $d_{\text{Selatan}} = 60\text{ m}$ dan sisi sampingnya adalah $d_{\text{Timur}} = 80\text{ m}$:

$$
\tan \theta = \frac{d_{\text{Selatan}}}{d_{\text{Timur}}} = \frac{60\text{ m}}{80\text{ m}} = \frac{3}{4} = 0,75
$$

Dari tabel nilai trigonometri, $\tan \theta = \frac{3}{4}$ bersesuaian dengan $\theta = 37^{\circ}$. Maka arah perpindahannya adalah $37^{\circ}$ *south of east* (atau jika diukur dari Selatan ke Timur adalah $90^{\circ} - 37^{\circ} = 53^{\circ}$ *east of south*).

**Jawaban: D**

**Soal 3:**
Two displacement vectors have the same magnitude of $S$ meters. If the magnitude of their resultant vector is also $S$ meters, the angle between the two vectors is...

A. $30^{\circ}$
B. $45^{\circ}$
C. $60^{\circ}$
D. $90^{\circ}$
E. $120^{\circ}$

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** $S_{1} = S$, $S_{2} = S$, dan besar resultan $R = S$.
* **Langkah Perhitungan dengan Rumus Kosinus:**

$$
R^{2} = S_{1}^{2} + S_{2}^{2} + 2 \cdot S_{1} \cdot S_{2} \cdot \cos \theta
$$

$$
S^{2} = S^{2} + S^{2} + 2 \cdot S \cdot S \cdot \cos \theta
$$

$$
S^{2} = 2S^{2} + 2S^{2} \cos \theta
$$

Kurangkan kedua ruas dengan $2S^{2}$:

$$
-S^{2} = 2S^{2} \cos \theta \Rightarrow \cos \theta = -\frac{S^{2}}{2S^{2}} = -\frac{1}{2} = -0,5
$$

* **Kesimpulan Sudut:** Nilai kosinus negatif menunjukkan bahwa sudut apit kedua vektor berada di Kuadran II (sudut tumpul). Sudut yang memenuhi $\cos \theta = -\frac{1}{2}$ adalah $\theta = 180^{\circ} - 60^{\circ} = 120^{\circ}$.

**Jawaban: E**

**Soal 4:**
Two forces acting on a common point have magnitudes of $16\text{ N}$ and $10\text{ N}$, respectively. If the magnitude of their resultant force is $14\text{ N}$, the angle between the two forces is...

A. $30^{\circ}$
B. $45^{\circ}$
C. $60^{\circ}$
D. $90^{\circ}$
E. $120^{\circ}$

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** $F_{1} = 16\text{ N}$, $F_{2} = 10\text{ N}$, dan resultan $R = 14\text{ N}$.
* **Langkah Perhitungan dengan Rumus Kosinus:**

$$
R^{2} = F_{1}^{2} + F_{2}^{2} + 2 \cdot F_{1} \cdot F_{2} \cdot \cos \theta
$$

$$
14^{2} = 16^{2} + 10^{2} + 2 \cdot (16) \cdot (10) \cdot \cos \theta
$$

$$
196 = 256 + 100 + 320 \cos \theta
$$

$$
196 = 356 + 320 \cos \theta
$$

$$
196 - 356 = 320 \cos \theta \Rightarrow -160 = 320 \cos \theta
$$

$$
\cos \theta = -\frac{160}{320} = -\frac{1}{2} = -0,5
$$

* **Kesimpulan Sudut:** Karena $\cos \theta = -\frac{1}{2}$, maka sudut apit antara kedua vektor gaya tersebut adalah $\theta = 120^{\circ}$.

**Jawaban: E**

**Soal 5:**
A velocity vector with a magnitude of $v = 40\text{ m/s}$ makes an angle of $30^{\circ}$ with the positive x-axis ($+x$). The components of this velocity vector are...

A. $v_{x} = 20\text{ m/s}$ and $v_{y} = 40\text{ m/s}$
B. $v_{x} = 20\text{ m/s}$ and $v_{y} = 20\text{ m/s}$
C. $v_{x} = 20\text{ m/s}$ and $v_{y} = 20\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$
D. $v_{x} = 20\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$ and $v_{y} = 20\text{ m/s}$
E. $v_{x} = 20\sqrt{2}\text{ m/s}$ and $v_{y} = 20\sqrt{2}\text{ m/s}$

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** Besar kecepatan $v = 40\text{ m/s}$ dengan sudut $\theta = 30^{\circ}$ terhadap sumbu $+x$.
* **Perhitungan Komponen Sumbu-**$x$ **dan Sumbu-**$y$**:**

$$
v_{x} = v \cos 30^{\circ} = 40\text{ m/s} \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = 20\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}
$$

$$
v_{y} = v \sin 30^{\circ} = 40\text{ m/s} \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = 20\text{ m/s}
$$

B. $v_{x} = -5\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$, $v_{y} = -5\text{ m/s}$

$$

$$
v_{y} = +v \sin 30^{\circ} = +10\text{ m/s} \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = +5\text{ m/s}

**Soal 8:**
Three coplanar force vectors act on a single point at the origin $(0, 0)$:

* $F_{1} = 150\text{ N}$ directed along the positive x-axis ($+x$),
* $F_{2} = 80\sqrt{3}\text{ N}$ in the second quadrant, making an angle of $30^{\circ}$ above the negative x-axis ($-x$),
* $F_{3} = 80\text{ N}$ in the third quadrant, making an angle of $60^{\circ}$ below the negative x-axis ($-x$).

The resultant of these three vectors is...

A. $10\text{ N}$ along the negative x-axis ($-x$)
B. $10\text{ N}$ along the positive x-axis ($+x$)
C. $20\text{ N}$ along the negative x-axis ($-x$)
D. $20\text{ N}$ along the positive x-axis ($+x$)
E. $30\text{ N}$ along the negative x-axis ($-x$)

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Penguraian Komponen Masing-Masing Vektor Gaya:**
  * Gaya 1 ($F_{1} = 150\text{ N}$ searah sumbu $+x$):

$$
F_{1x} = +150\text{ N} \quad \text{dan} \quad F_{1y} = 0\text{ N}
$$

  * Gaya 2 ($F_{2} = 80\sqrt{3}\text{ N}$ di Kuadran II, sudut $30^{\circ}$ di atas sumbu $-x$):

$$
F_{2x} = -F_{2} \cos 30^{\circ} = -80\sqrt{3} \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = -40 \times 3 = -120\text{ N}
$$

$$
F_{2y} = +F_{2} \sin 30^{\circ} = +80\sqrt{3} \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = +40\sqrt{3}\text{ N}
$$

  * Gaya 3 ($F_{3} = 80\text{ N}$ di Kuadran III, sudut $60^{\circ}$ di bawah sumbu $-x$):

$$
F_{3x} = -F_{3} \cos 60^{\circ} = -80 \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = -40\text{ N}
$$

$$
F_{3y} = -F_{3} \sin 60^{\circ} = -80 \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = -40\sqrt{3}\text{ N}
$$

* **Penjumlahan Komponen pada Sumbu-**$x$ **dan Sumbu-**$y$**:**

$$
\Sigma F_{x} = F_{1x} + F_{2x} + F_{3x} = 150\text{ N} + (-120\text{ N}) + (-40\text{ N}) = -10\text{ N}
$$

$$
\Sigma F_{y} = F_{1y} + F_{2y} + F_{3y} = 0\text{ N} + 40\sqrt{3}\text{ N} + (-40\sqrt{3}\text{ N}) = 0\text{ N}
$$

* **Kesimpulan Resultan:** Karena $\Sigma F_{x} = -10\text{ N}$ dan $\Sigma F_{y} = 0\text{ N}$, maka vektor resultannya bernilai $10\text{ N}$ mengarah sepanjang sumbu-$x$ negatif ($-x$).

**Jawaban: A**

**Soal 9:**
Kevin walks $32\text{ m}$ due East, then turns by an angle of $143^{\circ}$ ($\sin 37^{\circ} = 0.6$) from East toward West (counter-clockwise) and walks $20\text{ m}$ before stopping.
Based on this data:

1. The northward component of Kevin's displacement is $12\text{ m}$.
2. The eastward component of Kevin's displacement is $16\text{ m}$.
3. Kevin's resultant displacement from the starting point is $20\text{ m}$.
4. Kevin's resultant displacement from the starting point is $28\text{ m}$.
5. The direction of Kevin's displacement is $37^{\circ}$ North of East.

The correct statements are...

A. 1 and 2
B. 4 and 5
C. 3 and 5
D. 1, 2, and 4
E. 1, 2, 3, and 5

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Tetapkan Sumbu Koordinat:** Arah Timur (*East*) sebagai sumbu $+x$ dan arah Utara (*North*) sebagai sumbu $+y$.
* **Perpindahan Pertama (**$d_{1} = 32\text{ m}$ **ke Timur):**

$$
d_{1x} = +32\text{ m} \quad \text{dan} \quad d_{1y} = 0\text{ m}
$$

* **Perpindahan Kedua (**$d_{2} = 20\text{ m}$ **berputar** $143^{\circ}$ **berlawanan arah jarum jam dari Timur):**
  Sudut $143^{\circ}$ dari sumbu $+x$ (Timur) membentuk sudut lancip $\alpha = 180^{\circ} - 143^{\circ} = 37^{\circ}$ terhadap sumbu $-x$ (arah $37^{\circ}$ Utara dari Barat, Kuadran II):

$$
d_{2x} = -d_{2} \cos 37^{\circ} = -20\text{ m} \times 0,8 = -16\text{ m} \text{ (ke Barat)}
$$

$$
d_{2y} = +d_{2} \sin 37^{\circ} = +20\text{ m} \times 0,6 = +12\text{ m} \text{ (ke Utara)}
$$

* **Evaluasi Kelima Pernyataan:**
  1. Komponen arah Utara (*Northward component*): $R_{y} = d_{1y} + d_{2y} = 0 + 12\text{ m} = 12\text{ m}$ (Pernyataan 1 BENAR).
  2. Komponen arah Timur (*Eastward component*): $R_{x} = d_{1x} + d_{2x} = 32\text{ m} - 16\text{ m} = 16\text{ m}$ (Pernyataan 2 BENAR).
  3. Besar perpindahan resultan: $R = \sqrt{R_{x}^{2} + R_{y}^{2}} = \sqrt{16^{2} + 12^{2}} = \sqrt{256 + 144} = \sqrt{400} = 20\text{ m}$ (Pernyataan 3 BENAR).
  4. Besar perpindahan resultan adalah $28\text{ m}$: Tidak sesuai dengan hasil $20\text{ m}$ (Pernyataan 4 SALAH).
  5. Arah perpindahan resultan: $\tan \theta = \frac{R_{y}}{R_{x}} = \frac{12}{16} = \frac{3}{4} = 0,75$ sehingga $\theta = 37^{\circ}$ ke Utara dari Timur ($37^{\circ}$ North of East) (Pernyataan 5 BENAR).

**Jawaban: E**

**Soal 10:**
A child is playing with a remote control car while seated. The toy car, initially at rest near the child's feet, is driven $5\text{ m}$ due East, then steered $5\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$ due South, and finally turned $10\text{ m}$ due West before stopping. The magnitude of the car's resultant displacement from the child's seated position is...

A. $5\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$
B. $8\text{ m}$
C. $10\text{ m}$
D. $20\text{ m}$
E. $25\text{ m}$

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** Tiga perpindahan berurutan: $5\text{ m}$ ke Timur ($+x$), $5\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$ ke Selatan ($-y$), dan $10\text{ m}$ ke Barat ($-x$).
* **Perhitungan Komponen Resultan:**
  * Komponen Timur-Barat ($x$): $R_{x} = +5\text{ m} - 10\text{ m} = -5\text{ m}$ ($5\text{ m}$ ke Barat)
  * Komponen Utara-Selatan ($y$): $R_{y} = -5\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$ ($5\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$ ke Selatan)
* **Perhitungan Besar Resultan:**

$$
R = \sqrt{R_{x}^{2} + R_{y}^{2}} = \sqrt{(-5)^{2} + (-5\sqrt{3})^{2}} = \sqrt{25 + 25 \times 3} = \sqrt{25 + 75} = \sqrt{100} = 10\text{ m}
$$

**Soal 12:**
A kayaker crosses a $60\text{ m}$ wide river by paddling at a constant speed of $3\text{ m/s}$ relative to the water, keeping the kayak directed perpendicular to the riverbank (due North). Due to the river current flowing East, the kayaker reaches the opposite bank drifted $80\text{ m}$ downstream (to the East) from the point directly opposite the starting position. The speed of the river current and the kayaker's resultant speed relative to the ground are, respectively...

A. $2\text{ m/s}$ and $5\text{ m/s}$
B. $3\text{ m/s}$ and $4\text{ m/s}$
C. $3\text{ m/s}$ and $5\text{ m/s}$
D. $4\text{ m/s}$ and $5\text{ m/s}$
E. $4\text{ m/s}$ and $7\text{ m/s}$

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** Lebar sungai $\Delta y = 60\text{ m}$ (arah Utara), kelajuan kayak terhadap air $v_{y} = 3\text{ m/s}$ (arah Utara), dan jarak pergeseran ke hilir $\Delta x = 80\text{ m}$ (arah Timur).
* **Langkah 1 - Hitung Waktu Penyeberangan (**$t$**):**

$$
t = \frac{\Delta y}{v_{y}} = \frac{60\text{ m}}{3\text{ m/s}} = 20\text{ s}
$$

* **Langkah 2 - Hitung Laju Arus Sungai (**$v_{x}$**):**
  Selama $t = 20\text{ s}$, arus menyeret kayak sejauh $\Delta x = 80\text{ m}$ ke Timur:

$$
v_{x} = \frac{\Delta x}{t} = \frac{80\text{ m}}{20\text{ s}} = 4\text{ m/s}
$$

* **Langkah 3 - Hitung Laju Resultan Kayak terhadap Tanah (**$v_{R}$**):**

$$
v_{R} = \sqrt{v_{x}^{2} + v_{y}^{2}} = \sqrt{(4\text{ m/s})^{2} + (3\text{ m/s})^{2}} = \sqrt{16 + 9} = \sqrt{25} = 5\text{ m/s}
$$

**Soal 22:**
If two non-zero vectors are perpendicular to each other, their dot product (scalar product) is...

C. Zero

---
**Pembahasan:**
Dua vektor yang saling tegak lurus (*perpendicular*) membentuk sudut apit $\theta = 90^{\circ}$. **Soal 23:**
When subtracting vector $B$ from vector $A$ graphically ($R = A - B$) using the head-to-tail method, how is the resultant vector $R$ constructed?

A. Place the tail of $B$ at the head of $A$, and draw $R$ from the tail of $A$ to the head of $B$.
B. Reverse $B$ to obtain $-B$, place the tail of $-B$ at the head of $A$, and draw $R$ from the tail of $A$ to the head of $-B$.
C. Connect the midpoints of vector $A$ and vector $B$ with a perpendicular line.
D. Reverse both $A$ and $B$, and connect their arrowheads together.
E. Place the head of $B$ at the tail of $A$, and draw $R$ from the head of $A$ to the tail of $B$.

---
**Pembahasan:**
Operasi pengurangan vektor $R = A - B$ secara aljabar vektor didefinisikan sebagai penjumlahan vektor $A$ dengan vektor negatif $-B$:

$$
R = A + (-B)
$$

Oleh sebab itu, langkah konstruksi menggunakan metode *head-to-tail* adalah:

1. Balikkan arah vektor $B$ sebesar $180^{\circ}$ untuk memperoleh vektor negatif $-B$.
2. Tempatkan pangkal (*tail*) dari vektor $-B$ tepat pada ujung panah (*head*) dari vektor $A$.
3. Tarik vektor resultan $R$ dari pangkal (*tail*) vektor $A$ menuju ujung panah (*head*) vektor $-B$.

**Jawaban: B**

**Soal 30:**
Referring to the same three vectors in Question 29 ($b$ due North, $c$ due West, and $a$ Northwest), which polygon (head-to-tail) arrangement correctly represents the negative vector equation $-a = -b - c$?

A. Draw $-b$ (due South), place the tail of $-c$ (due East) at the head of $-b$, and draw resultant $-a$ (Southeast) from the tail of $-b$ to the head of $-c$.
B. Draw $b$ (due North), place the tail of $-c$ (due East) at the head of $b$, and draw $-a$ from the tail of $b$ to the head of $-c$.
