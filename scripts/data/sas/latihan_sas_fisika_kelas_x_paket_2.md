**Soal 1:**
Two force vectors acting on an object have magnitudes of $12\text{ N}$ and $5\text{ N}$, respectively. Which of the following CANNOT be the magnitude of their resultant force?

A. $6\text{ N}$
B. $7\text{ N}$
C. $10\text{ N}$
D. $13\text{ N}$
E. $17\text{ N}$

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** Besar dua gaya yang bekerja pada benda adalah $F_{1} = 12\text{ N}$ dan $F_{2} = 5\text{ N}$.
* **Konsep Fisika:** Besar resultan ($R$) dua vektor dibatasi oleh nilai minimum saat keduanya berlawanan arah ($\theta = 180^{\circ}$) dan nilai maksimum saat keduanya searah ($\theta = 0^{\circ}$).
* **Perhitungan Batas Nilai Resultan:**

$$
R_{\min} = \vert{}F_{1} - F_{2}\vert{} = \vert{}12\text{ N} - 5\text{ N}\vert{} = 7\text{ N}
$$

$$
R_{\max} = F_{1} + F_{2} = 12\text{ N} + 5\text{ N} = 17\text{ N}
$$

* **Evaluasi Pilihan:** Nilai resultan harus berada dalam interval $7\text{ N} \le R \le 17\text{ N}$. Nilai $7\text{ N}$, $10\text{ N}$, $13\text{ N}$, dan $17\text{ N}$ mungkin dihasilkan, sedangkan $6\text{ N}$ berada di bawah batas minimum ($6\text{ N} < 7\text{ N}$) sehingga tidak mungkin menjadi besar resultan kedua gaya tersebut.

**Jawaban: A**

**Soal 2:**
A cyclist rides $40\text{ m}$ to the West, then turns North and rides $30\text{ m}$ before stopping. The direction of the cyclist's displacement from the starting point is...

A. $30^{\circ}$ North of West
B. $37^{\circ}$ South of West
C. $37^{\circ}$ North of West
D. $53^{\circ}$ North of West
E. $60^{\circ}$ West of North

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** Perpindahan pertama ke Barat $d_{\text{Barat}} = 40\text{ m}$ (sumbu $-x$) dan perpindahan kedua ke Utara $d_{\text{Utara}} = 30\text{ m}$ (sumbu $+y$). Posisi akhir berada di Kuadran II (antara Barat dan Utara).
* **Perhitungan Sudut Arah terhadap Sumbu Barat ($\theta_{W}$):**
  Sudut $\theta_{W}$ yang diukur dari arah Barat menuju arah Utara (*North of West*) memiliki sisi depan $d_{\text{Utara}} = 30\text{ m}$ dan sisi samping $d_{\text{Barat}} = 40\text{ m}$:

$$
\tan \theta_{W} = \frac{d_{\text{Utara}}}{d_{\text{Barat}}} = \frac{30\text{ m}}{40\text{ m}} = \frac{3}{4} = 0,75
$$

* **Kesimpulan Arah:** Berdasarkan tabel trigonometri, nilai $\tan \theta_{W} = \frac{3}{4}$ memberikan sudut $\theta_{W} = 37^{\circ}$. Karena perpindahan mengarah ke Utara dari garis Barat, maka arahnya adalah $37^{\circ}$ North of West.

**Jawaban: C**

**Soal 3:**
Two force vectors have the exact same magnitude of $F$ Newtons. If the magnitude of their resultant vector is $\sqrt{2}F$ Newtons, the angle between the two vectors is...

A. $30^{\circ}$
B. $45^{\circ}$
C. $60^{\circ}$
D. $90^{\circ}$
E. $120^{\circ}$

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** $F_{1} = F$, $F_{2} = F$, dan besar resultan $R = \sqrt{2}F$.
* **Substitusi ke dalam Rumus Kosinus:**

$$
R^{2} = F_{1}^{2} + F_{2}^{2} + 2 \cdot F_{1} \cdot F_{2} \cdot \cos \theta
$$

$$
(\sqrt{2}F)^{2} = F^{2} + F^{2} + 2 \cdot F \cdot F \cdot \cos \theta
$$

$$
2F^{2} = 2F^{2} + 2F^{2} \cos \theta
$$

Kurangkan kedua ruas dengan $2F^{2}$:

$$
0 = 2F^{2} \cos \theta \Rightarrow \cos \theta = 0
$$

* **Kesimpulan Sudut:** Nilai sudut yang memenuhi $\cos \theta = 0$ adalah $\theta = 90^{\circ}$ (kedua vektor saling tegak lurus).

**Jawaban: D**

**Soal 4:**
Two forces have magnitudes of $20\text{ N}$ and $10\text{ N}$, respectively. If the magnitude of their resultant force is $10\sqrt{7}\text{ N}$, the angle between the two forces is...

A. $30^{\circ}$
B. $45^{\circ}$
C. $60^{\circ}$
D. $90^{\circ}$
E. $120^{\circ}$

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** $F_{1} = 20\text{ N}$, $F_{2} = 10\text{ N}$, dan resultan $R = 10\sqrt{7}\text{ N}$.
* **Langkah Perhitungan dengan Rumus Kosinus:**

$$
R^{2} = F_{1}^{2} + F_{2}^{2} + 2 \cdot F_{1} \cdot F_{2} \cdot \cos \theta
$$

$$
(10\sqrt{7})^{2} = 20^{2} + 10^{2} + 2 \cdot (20) \cdot (10) \cdot \cos \theta
$$

$$
100 \times 7 = 400 + 100 + 400 \cos \theta
$$

$$
700 = 500 + 400 \cos \theta
$$

$$
700 - 500 = 400 \cos \theta \Rightarrow 200 = 400 \cos \theta
$$

$$
\cos \theta = \frac{200}{400} = \frac{1}{2} = 0,5
$$

* **Kesimpulan Sudut:** Sudut lancip yang nilai kosinusnya $\frac{1}{2}$ adalah $\theta = 60^{\circ}$.

**Jawaban: C**

**Soal 5:**

B. $v_{x} = 15\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$ and $v_{y} = 15\text{ m/s}$

E. $v_{x} = 30\text{ m/s}$ and $v_{y} = 15\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** Besar kecepatan $v = 30\text{ m/s}$ dengan sudut $\theta = 60^{\circ}$ terhadap sumbu $+x$ (Kuadran I, semua komponen bertanda positif).
* **Perhitungan Komponen Sumbu-$x$ dan Sumbu-$y$:**

$$
v_{x} = v \cos 60^{\circ} = 30\text{ m/s} \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = 15\text{ m/s}

v_{y} = v \sin 60^{\circ} = 30\text{ m/s} \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = 15\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}

A displacement vector $S$ has a magnitude of $15\text{ m}$ and lies in the second quadrant, making an angle of $53^{\circ}$ above the negative x-axis. The components of vector $S$ are...

**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** Besar perpindahan $S = 15\text{ m}$ di Kuadran II, membentuk sudut $\alpha = 53^{\circ}$ di atas sumbu $-x$ ($\cos 53^{\circ} = \frac{3}{5} = 0,6$ dan $\sin 53^{\circ} = \frac{4}{5} = 0,8$).
* **Analisis Tanda Kuadran II:** Pada kuadran kedua, proyeksi horizontal mengarah ke sumbu $-x$ ($S_{x}$ negatif) dan proyeksi vertikal mengarah ke sumbu $+y$ ($S_{y}$ positif).

**Soal 7:**
A velocity vector has a magnitude of $14\text{ m/s}$ and is directed at an angle of $-240^{\circ}$ (measured clockwise) relative to the positive x-axis. The components of the vector are...

A. $v_{x} = -7\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$, $v_{y} = 7\text{ m/s}$
B. $v_{x} = -7\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$, $v_{y} = -7\text{ m/s}$
C. $v_{x} = -7\text{ m/s}$, $v_{y} = 7\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$
D. $v_{x} = -7\text{ m/s}$, $v_{y} = -7\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}$

---

Dengan kata lain, sudut $-240^{\circ}$ ekuivalen dengan sudut $360^{\circ} - 240^{\circ} = +120^{\circ}$ berlawanan arah jarum jam, sehingga vektor terletak di Kuadran II dengan sudut referensi $\alpha = 60^{\circ}$ terhadap sumbu $-x$.

$$
v_{x} = -v \cos 60^{\circ} = -14\text{ m/s} \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = -7\text{ m/s}
$$

$$
v_{y} = +v \sin 60^{\circ} = +14\text{ m/s} \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = +7\sqrt{3}\text{ m/s}
$$

**Soal 8:**
Three coplanar force vectors act on a single point at the origin $(0, 0)$:

* $F_{1} = 80\text{ N}$ directed along the positive x-axis ($+x$),
* $F_{2} = 50\sqrt{3}\text{ N}$ in the second quadrant, making an angle of $30^{\circ}$ above the negative x-axis ($-x$),
* $F_{3} = 50\text{ N}$ in the third quadrant, making an angle of $60^{\circ}$ below the negative x-axis ($-x$).

The resultant of these three vectors is...

A. $10\text{ N}$ along the positive x-axis ($+x$)
B. $20\text{ N}$ along the positive x-axis ($+x$)
C. $20\text{ N}$ along the negative x-axis ($-x$)
D. $30\text{ N}$ along the negative x-axis ($-x$)
E. $30\text{ N}$ along the positive x-axis ($+x$)

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Uraikan Komponen Masing-Masing Vektor Gaya terhadap Sumbu-$x$ dan Sumbu-$y$:**

  * Gaya 1 ($F_{1} = 80\text{ N}$ searah sumbu $+x$):

$$
F_{1x} = +80\text{ N} \quad \text{dan} \quad F_{1y} = 0\text{ N}
$$

  * Gaya 2 ($F_{2} = 50\sqrt{3}\text{ N}$ di Kuadran II, sudut $30^{\circ}$ di atas sumbu $-x$):

$$
F_{2x} = -F_{2} \cos 30^{\circ} = -50\sqrt{3} \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = -25 \times 3 = -75\text{ N}
$$

$$
F_{2y} = +F_{2} \sin 30^{\circ} = +50\sqrt{3} \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = +25\sqrt{3}\text{ N}
$$

  * Gaya 3 ($F_{3} = 50\text{ N}$ di Kuadran III, sudut $60^{\circ}$ di bawah sumbu $-x$):

$$
F_{3x} = -F_{3} \cos 60^{\circ} = -50 \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = -25\text{ N}
$$

$$
F_{3y} = -F_{3} \sin 60^{\circ} = -50 \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = -25\sqrt{3}\text{ N}
$$

* **Jumlahkan Komponen Searah Sumbu-$x$ dan Sumbu-$y$:**

$$
\Sigma F_{x} = F_{1x} + F_{2x} + F_{3x} = 80\text{ N} + (-75\text{ N}) + (-25\text{ N}) = -20\text{ N}
$$

$$
\Sigma F_{y} = F_{1y} + F_{2y} + F_{3y} = 0\text{ N} + 25\sqrt{3}\text{ N} + (-25\sqrt{3}\text{ N}) = 0\text{ N}
$$

* **Kesimpulan Resultan:** Karena $\Sigma F_{y} = 0\text{ N}$ dan $\Sigma F_{x} = -20\text{ N}$, maka resultan ketiga gaya tersebut memiliki besar $20\text{ N}$ dan mengarah sepanjang sumbu-$x$ negatif ($-x$).

**Jawaban: C**

**Soal 9:**
Arnov walks $16\text{ m}$ due East, then turns by an angle of $143^{\circ}$ ($\sin 37^{\circ} = 0.6$) from East toward West (counter-clockwise) and walks $10\text{ m}$ before stopping.
Based on this data:

1. The northward component of Arnov's displacement is $6\text{ m}$.
2. The eastward component of Arnov's displacement is $8\text{ m}$.
3. Arnov's resultant displacement from the starting point is $10\text{ m}$.
4. Arnov's resultant displacement from the starting point is $14\text{ m}$.
5. The direction of Arnov's displacement is $37^{\circ}$ North of East.

The correct statements are...

A. 1 and 2
B. 4 and 5
C. 3 and 5
D. 1, 2, and 4
E. 1, 2, 3, and 5

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Tetapkan Sistem Koordinat:** Timur (*East*) sebagai sumbu $+x$ dan Utara (*North*) sebagai sumbu $+y$.
* **Perpindahan Tahap 1 ($d_{1} = 16\text{ m}$ ke Timur):**

$$
d_{1x} = +16\text{ m} \quad \text{dan} \quad d_{1y} = 0\text{ m}
$$

* **Perpindahan Tahap 2 ($d_{2} = 10\text{ m}$ dengan sudut putar $143^{\circ}$ berlawanan arah jarum jam dari Timur):**
  Berputar $143^{\circ}$ dari arah Timur ($0^{\circ}$) menuju Barat ($180^{\circ}$) menempatkan lintasan kedua di Kuadran II dengan sudut lancip $\alpha = 180^{\circ} - 143^{\circ} = 37^{\circ}$ di atas sumbu Barat ($-x$):

$$
d_{2x} = -d_{2} \cos 37^{\circ} = -10\text{ m} \times 0,8 = -8\text{ m} \text{ (ke Barat)}
$$

$$
d_{2y} = +d_{2} \sin 37^{\circ} = +10\text{ m} \times 0,6 = +6\text{ m} \text{ (ke Utara)}
$$

* **Uji Kebenaran Kelima Pernyataan:**
  1. Komponen arah Utara (*Northward component*): $R_{y} = d_{1y} + d_{2y} = 0 + 6\text{ m} = 6\text{ m}$ (Pernyataan 1 BENAR).
  2. Komponen arah Timur (*Eastward component*): $R_{x} = d_{1x} + d_{2x} = 16\text{ m} - 8\text{ m} = 8\text{ m}$ (Pernyataan 2 BENAR).
  3. Besar perpindahan resultan: $R = \sqrt{R_{x}^{2} + R_{y}^{2}} = \sqrt{8^{2} + 6^{2}} = \sqrt{64 + 36} = \sqrt{100} = 10\text{ m}$ (Pernyataan 3 BENAR).
  4. Besar perpindahan resultan adalah $14\text{ m}$: Salah, karena hasil sebenarnya adalah $10\text{ m}$ (Pernyataan 4 SALAH).
  5. Arah perpindahan resultan: Karena $R_{x} = +8\text{ m}$ (Timur) dan $R_{y} = +6\text{ m}$ (Utara), maka $\tan \theta = \frac{R_{y}}{R_{x}} = \frac{6}{8} = \frac{3}{4} = 0,75$ sehingga $\theta = 37^{\circ}$ ke Utara dari Timur ($37^{\circ}$ North of East) (Pernyataan 5 BENAR).

**Jawaban: E**

**Soal 10:**
A child is operating a remote-controlled boat on a calm pond. Starting from the dock, the boat is steered $6\text{ m}$ due East, then turned $6\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$ due North, and finally steered $12\text{ m}$ due West before stopping. The magnitude of the boat's resultant displacement from the dock is...

A. $6\text{ m}$
B. $6\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$
C. $12\text{ m}$
D. $18\text{ m}$
E. $24\text{ m}$

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** Tiga perpindahan berurutan: $6\text{ m}$ ke Timur ($+x$), $6\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$ ke Utara ($+y$), dan $12\text{ m}$ ke Barat ($-x$).
* **Perhitungan Komponen Resultan:**
  * Sumbu Timur-Barat ($x$): $R_{x} = +6\text{ m} - 12\text{ m} = -6\text{ m}$ ($6\text{ m}$ ke arah Barat)
  * Sumbu Utara-Selatan ($y$): $R_{y} = +6\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$ (ke arah Utara)
* **Perhitungan Besar Resultan:**

$$
R = \sqrt{R_{x}^{2} + R_{y}^{2}} = \sqrt{(-6)^{2} + (6\sqrt{3})^{2}} = \sqrt{36 + 36 \times 3} = \sqrt{36 + 108} = \sqrt{144} = 12\text{ m}
$$

**Soal 12:**
A swimmer crosses an $80\text{ m}$ wide river by swimming at a constant speed of $4\text{ m/s}$ relative to the water, keeping their body directed perpendicular to the riverbank (due North). Due to the river current flowing East, the swimmer reaches the opposite bank drifted $60\text{ m}$ downstream (to the East) from the point directly opposite the starting position. The speed of the river current and the swimmer's resultant speed relative to the ground are, respectively...

A. $2\text{ m/s}$ and $4\sqrt{2}\text{ m/s}$
B. $3\text{ m/s}$ and $5\text{ m/s}$
C. $3\text{ m/s}$ and $7\text{ m/s}$
D. $4\text{ m/s}$ and $5\text{ m/s}$
E. $5\text{ m/s}$ and $6\text{ m/s}$

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Diketahui:** Lebar sungai $\Delta y = 80\text{ m}$ (arah Utara), kelajuan renang terhadap air $v_{y} = 4\text{ m/s}$ (arah Utara), dan jarak hanyut ke hilir $\Delta x = 60\text{ m}$ (arah Timur).
* **Langkah 1 - Hitung Waktu Tempuh Menyeberangi Sungai ($t$):**

$$
t = \frac{\Delta y}{v_{y}} = \frac{80\text{ m}}{4\text{ m/s}} = 20\text{ s}
$$

* **Langkah 2 - Hitung Kelajuan Arus Sungai ($v_{x}$):**
  Dalam waktu $t = 20\text{ s}$, perenang terdorong ke Timur sejauh $\Delta x = 60\text{ m}$:

$$
v_{x} = \frac{\Delta x}{t} = \frac{60\text{ m}}{20\text{ s}} = 3\text{ m/s}
$$

* **Langkah 3 - Hitung Kelajuan Resultan Perenang terhadap Tanah ($v_{R}$):**
  Karena vektor kecepatan renang ($v_{y} = 4\text{ m/s}$ Utara) dan kecepatan arus ($v_{x} = 3\text{ m/s}$ Timur) saling tegak lurus:

$$
v_{R} = \sqrt{v_{x}^{2} + v_{y}^{2}} = \sqrt{(3\text{ m/s})^{2} + (4\text{ m/s})^{2}} = \sqrt{9 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5\text{ m/s}
$$

**Jawaban: B**

**Soal 22:**
If the dot product (scalar product) of two non-zero vectors $A$ and $B$ is equal to zero ($A \cdot B = 0$), what is the angle between the two vectors?

A. $0^{\circ}$
B. $30^{\circ}$
C. $45^{\circ}$
D. $90^{\circ}$
E. $180^{\circ}$

---
**Pembahasan:**
Persamaan *dot product* adalah $A \cdot B = \vert{}A\vert{} \cdot \vert{}B\vert{} \cdot \cos \theta$. Diketahui bahwa $A$ dan $B$ adalah vektor tak-nol ($\vert{}A\vert{} \neq 0$ dan $\vert{}B\vert{} \neq 0$) serta $A \cdot B = 0$. Maka haruslah $\cos \theta = 0$, yang berarti sudut apit antara kedua vektor tersebut adalah $90^{\circ}$ (saling tegak lurus atau ortogonal).

**Soal 29:**
Given three vectors: vector $p$ points due South, vector $q$ points due East, and vector $r$ points Southeast. B. $q = p + r$

D. $r = p - q$

**Pembahasan:**

* Vektor $r$ (ke Tenggara / *Southeast*) ditarik dari pangkal vektor pertama ($p$) menuju ujung kepala vektor terakhir ($q$), sehingga $r$ adalah resultan dari $p + q$.
* Dengan demikian, persamaan vektor yang tepat adalah $r = p + q$.

**Soal 30:**
Referring to the same three vectors ($p$ due South, $q$ due East, and $r$ Southeast), if a student draws vector $-p$ (due North), places the tail of $-q$ (due West) at the head of $-p$, and draws the resultant vector from the tail of $-p$ to the head of $-q$, which vector equation does this diagram represent?

A. $r = -p - q$ (directed Southeast)
B. $-r = -p - q$ (directed Northwest)
C. $-r = p - q$ (directed Southwest)
D. $r = p + q$ (directed Northeast)
E. $-r = -p + q$ (directed Northeast)

---
**Pembahasan:**

* **Analisis Penjumlahan Vektor yang Digambar:**
  Siswa menggambar vektor $-p$ (berarah ke Utara, lawan dari Selatan) kemudian menyambungkan pangkal vektor $-q$ (berarah ke Barat, lawan dari Timur) di ujung kepala $-p$. Operasi penjumlahan ini adalah $(-p) + (-q) = -p - q$.
* **Analisis Arah dan Nama Vektor Resultannya:**
  Kombinasi arah Utara ($-p$) dan arah Barat ($-q$) menghasilkan vektor yang mengarah ke Barat Laut (*Northwest*). Karena vektor $r = p + q$ berarah ke Tenggara (*Southeast*), maka vektor yang besarnya sama namun berarah berlawanan ($180^{\circ}$) ke Barat Laut adalah vektor negatifnya, yaitu:

$$
-r = -(p + q) = -p - q
$$

**Jawaban: B**
